Brand · Logo system

Two marks, one school.

The school emblem — with children, star, leaves, and the school name — is the primary logo. Use it on all print, physical, and large-format materials. The G medallion is the digital shorthand: navbar icons, app avatars, favicons, and UI elements below 64 px where the full emblem would lose its detail.
