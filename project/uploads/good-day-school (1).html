Home  /  About us

A small school. A long story. Twenty-five years and counting.

Good Day School began in 2001 in a single rented room with eleven children and three teachers. We are still small. We will stay small.

What we built is a campus on the edge of the city — technically a farmhouse, in truth a quiet world of its own. Mountain views to one side, gardens and open fields to the other. Children walk barefoot through grass between classes.

We are licensed under the Pakistan unified national curriculum for grades 1–5, with our own additions: Quran, Islamiyat, mother-training, and learning rehabilitation.

What we believe

Conventional and unconventional, in the same classroom.

روایتی طریقے سے

The conventional half

Multiplication tables. Handwriting. Qaida. Spelling tests. Reading aloud. Sitting properly. Greeting your teacher. Standing for the national anthem. We do not skip the basics. We never have.

غیر روایتی طریقے سے

The unconventional half

Storytelling instead of summary. Building before reading. Cooking the math. Walking the science. Different children learn differently and our teachers know how to bend without breaking the curriculum. After 25 years, this is craft, not theory.

محفوظ، حد بندی شدہ اے آئی

AI used carefully, never instead of teachers

Our internal AI assistant runs in a sandbox locked to our curriculum. It cannot answer outside it. Every reply is reviewed. We use it to lighten teacher paperwork — not to replace the teacher.

والدین — برادری کا حصّہ

Parents are not customers — they are partners

Mothers are trained alongside their children. Fathers are welcome at any time. Daily teacher notes go home every evening. WhatsApp lines stay open. The school is yours.

The team

Twelve teachers. Seven of them have been with us a decade or more.

12 full-time teachers

Seven Pakistani-trained, three Cambridge-certified, two specialists in learning difficulty.

Internal teacher KPIs

We measure what matters: child engagement, parent feedback, peer review, and growth across the year.

Continuous training

Every teacher attends an internal weekly training. Our system is built for cost-efficient, structured growth.

Daycare

A separate, quiet wing of the campus, just for the youngest. Soft floors, low windows, gardens at hand.

Kindergarten

Two full years that turn play into reading. Separate from daycare. Separate from primary.

KG 1 — the gentle year

Routines, language, song, and the joy of being in a group. We sing the alphabet in three languages. We count to twenty in two. We learn each other's names.

KG 2 — the readiness year

By the end of KG 2 a child should hold a pencil correctly, sound out simple words in Urdu and English, recite their first short surahs, and sit through a 25-minute lesson.

Daily rhythm

  • Morning circle & prayer (15 min)
  • Language activity — Urdu, English, Arabic rotation
  • Numeracy through play
  • Garden break and snack
  • Quran & story time
  • Art, music, or movement

Learning Rehabilitation

For the child who cannot keep up — and for the child the system gave up on.

Who this is for

  • Dyslexia, dysgraphia, dyscalculia
  • Speech and language delay
  • ADHD and attention difficulties
  • Autism spectrum (mild to moderate)
  • Anxiety, school refusal, slow processing

How it works

Each child gets a 12-week assessment first. Then a written, agreed plan signed by the specialist, the parent, and the head teacher. The mother joins our training program in parallel — that is non-negotiable, because the home matters more than the classroom.

A note from the head teacher

We have walked this road with hundreds of families over 25 years. We do not promise miracles. We promise patience, honesty, and a teacher who will not give up before you do.

Mother Training & Family Rehabilitation

Twelve weeks. One supervisor. Real change in the house, not just in the school.

The curriculum

  • Child development from 0 to 10 — what is normal, what is not
  • Reading at home: routines, books, and the read-aloud habit
  • Health, nutrition, sleep, and screen time
  • Emotional regulation — for the child and the mother
  • Basic financial literacy & home economics
  • Working with the school — what to ask, when to ask

Your supervisor

Each mother is paired with a supervisor — one of our experienced teachers. The supervisor is reachable on WhatsApp during school hours.

A graduation, with paper

At the end of the program, every mother who completes 80% of the sessions earns a written certificate from Good Day School.

AI used carefully — never instead of the teacher.

Our internal AI assistant is a tool. The teacher is the teacher.

What it does

  • Answers a child's curriculum questions in their preferred language
  • Pronounces Quran and Islamiyat passages in a live voice mode
  • Helps a teacher draft a child's monthly progress report
  • Helps a mother's supervisor turn home notes into structured updates

What it will not do

  • Answer outside the curriculum scope — it simply won't
  • Hold a private conversation with a child without a teacher review
  • Replace handwriting, mental math, or face-to-face teaching
  • Send the child's data anywhere outside our school's secure server

Data & safety

Children's interactions are logged on a private server, accessible only to the class teacher and the parent.
