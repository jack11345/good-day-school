Ustadha Kiran · Quran Hifz Teacher
Zainab has shown exceptional dedication to her Hifz journey this term. Her tajweed has improved markedly — particularly her application of Qalqala and Ghunna, which now flows naturally in her recitation. She has memorised 16 surahs from Juz Amma with strong accuracy and is working steadily through Surah Az-Zalzalah. Her Manzil consistency is impressive; she rarely misses a revision. I encourage the family to continue listening to her daily recitation at home — even 10 minutes each evening makes an enormous difference. May Allah bless Zainab with the blessing of the Quran in her heart and her life. Ameen.
