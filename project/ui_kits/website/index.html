Our story

A small school. A long story. Twenty-five years and counting.

Good Day School began in 2001 in a single rented room with eleven children and three teachers. We are still small. We will stay small.

What we built is a campus on the edge of the city — technically a farmhouse, in truth a quiet world of its own. Mountain views to one side, gardens and open fields to the other.

See our programs →
What we believe

Conventional and unconventional, in the same classroom.

روایتی طریقے سے

The conventional half

Multiplication tables. Handwriting. Qaida. Spelling tests. Reading aloud. Standing for the national anthem. We do not skip the basics. We never have.

غیر روایتی طریقے سے

The unconventional half

Storytelling instead of summary. Building before reading. Cooking the math. Walking the science. After 25 years, this is craft, not theory.

AI used carefully, never instead of teachers

Our internal AI runs in a sandbox locked to our curriculum. Every reply is reviewed. We use it to lighten paperwork — not to replace the teacher.

Parents are not customers — they are partners

Mothers are trained alongside their children. Fathers welcome at any time. Daily notes every evening. WhatsApp lines stay open. The school is yours.

Join us

Admissions are open for the 2026–2027 session.

We admit new students throughout the year, subject to availability. The best way to start is a campus visit — come and let your child decide.

1

Schedule a visit

Every Thursday and Saturday morning. Bring your child. Walk the campus, meet the teachers.

2

Assessment

A gentle, play-based assessment for younger children. A short reading and maths check for Grade 1+.

3

Offer letter

If we have a place, we issue a formal offer within 2 working days with the full fee schedule.

4

Enrol

Submit B-Form, CNICs, and documents. Pay admission and first month's fee. Your child starts.
