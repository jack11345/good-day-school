Color · Mountain & Earth

The palette is older than the school. Maroon for ceremony, gold for warmth, cream for paper.

Three colours do most of the work. We borrow another four tones for status, accent, and ink. Every UI surface is built from this small handful — we resist the urge to add.

Typography · Four Voices

Cormorant for the school's voice. Lora for its breath. DM Sans for its work. Nastaliq for its second tongue.

A serif for ceremony — diplomas, hero headlines, the wordmark. A book serif for daily notes and quotations. A sans for forms, navigation, and metadata. And Urdu set in Noto Nastaliq, sized up by half so it never feels like a translation.

Brand · Two marks, one identity

The school emblem with children is the logo. The G medallion is its digital shorthand.

The full logo — children, star, leaves, and the school seal — is the primary mark for print, gates, diplomas, and all large-format uses. The G medallion is the companion mark: used at small sizes in apps, navbars, and screen UI where the full emblem would lose its detail.
